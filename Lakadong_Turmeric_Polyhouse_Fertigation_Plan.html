3. Seed rhizomes and planting

Healthy, untreated mother or finger pieces of 25–30 g with 2–3 plump buds. Plant 4–5 cm deep, buds up, one piece per bag. Keep media just moist until shoots appear.

Seed treatment

Biological only: Pseudomonas fluorescens 10 g + Trichoderma viride 4 g per kg of rhizomes. Use only products your buyer or certifier has accepted, and record product name, batch number, date and quantity.

Not for this drip system: Yara Nitrabor and YaraMila. Nitrabor is a field-grade calcium nitrate with 0.3% boron and a coating for dry spreading; the coating can block drippers, and at 750 g / 1000 L it would give ≈ 2.25 ppm boron, about 6 times the target. YaraMila is a granular NPK for soil. Buy fertigation-grade, fully water-soluble products only.

Rule: never mix calcium nitrate with MKP, SOP or magnesium sulphate in concentrated form. Keep them in separate tanks A and B; they meet only after dilution.

Heavy metals: buy only fertigation or technical grade. Get a heavy-metal certificate (Pb, Cd, As, Hg) for every batch of every fertiliser, micronutrient salt and acid. Use nitric acid for pH correction; use phosphoric acid only if it is technical or food grade with a certificate. Keep the certificates with the input register.

5. When to start feeding

  1. Planting to emergence (≈ 0–25 DAP): no fertiliser. Plain water only, just enough to keep media moist.
  2. First feed: when the first leaf has unfolded on most plants. Use the Stage 1 recipe.
  3. From then on: fertiliser at every irrigation. No plain-water-only days.

How irrigation is steered

Water is given in small shots, and each decision is made from a root-zone meter (moisture and EC inside the bag, e.g. a Bluetooth pulse meter), not from drain. Very little water drains out, which keeps water and fertiliser use low.

Root-zone moistureKeep between 30% and 50%
Root-zone ECNever above 3.0 mS/cm
Where to measureThe same 10 marked bags every day, spread across the polyhouse (front, middle, back, bed edges and centres), at the same depth and the same distance from the dripper

  • Give a shot when the root zone has dried towards 30–35%. Skip it if moisture is still above 45–50%.
  • First shot 1–2 hours after sunrise; last shot at least 2–3 hours before sunset, so bags do not stay wet overnight.
  • Shot length depends on the dripper: 30 ml is about 55 seconds on a 2 L/h dripper, or about 27 seconds on a 4 L/h dripper. Set the controller in seconds, then confirm the volume with the check below.
  • Use pressure-compensating, non-leak (anti-drain) drippers so every bag gets the same amount and laterals stay full between shots.
  • Dripper check (monthly, and before planting): catch one shot from 10 drippers across the house in measuring cups. All should be within ±10% of the average; clean or replace any that are not.

12. Pest and disease control (no pesticides)

  • Insect-proof netting (40–50 mesh) on all vents and openings; repair tears immediately.
  • Double door entry with a footbath; doors kept closed.
  • Yellow and blue sticky traps across the polyhouse (about 20–40 per acre); check and record catches weekly.
  • Walk every bed once a week; look for thrips, scale insects, shoot borer and leaf spots.
  • Remove affected plants or leaves with their media, bag them and take them out of the polyhouse.
  • Keep the area around the polyhouse free of weeds; bring in no plant material other than the checked seed rhizomes.
  • Clean tools and harvest crates before use; separate tools for any bed with disease.
  • No chemical pesticide, fungicide or insecticide at any stage, including stored seed and harvested rhizomes. Any biological product only with buyer approval, recorded in the input register.

13. Polyhouse climate

  • Target 20–35 °C with high humidity.
  • April–May: keep side vents open; use 35–50% shade net over the young crop if temperature climbs above 35 °C; run foggers or fan-pads if installed.

14. Harvest and post-harvest

  1. Harvest when leaves have yellowed and dried and stems begin to lodge (≈ 250–270 DAP).
  2. Cut back dried tops, empty bags onto a clean tarpaulin, lift the clumps.
  3. Process (boil, slice, dry) within 2–3 days; until then store in shade under clean sawdust or coir dust.
  4. Boil in clean potable water in stainless steel vessels.
  5. Dry on clean raised trays or food-grade mats, never on the ground or roadside, until moisture is 8–10%.
  6. Pack in clean food-grade bags; store dry, off the floor, away from fertilisers and fuel.
  7. Keep the best disease-free mother rhizomes as seed for next season (no treatment).
  8. Send a sample of each lot to an accredited lab for pesticide multi-residue, heavy metals (Pb, Cd, As, Hg), aflatoxins and curcumin. Results must meet current EU limits — Regulation (EC) 396/2005 for pesticide residues and Regulation (EU) 2023/915 for contaminants.
